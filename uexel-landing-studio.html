Our Process

A clear, collaborative path from first conversation to shipped product — refined across 1100+ engagements.

01

Discovery

We start by listening — mapping your goals, users, and constraints before writing a single line of code.

  • Stakeholder interviews & technical audit
  • Success metrics and scope defined
  • Roadmap and timeline agreed
02

Planning

Every engagement gets an architecture and a plan built to hold up under real-world load.

  • Tech stack and system architecture decided
  • Sprint plan and milestones set
  • Risk areas flagged early
03

Design

We prototype fast and validate early, so the product feels right before it's fully built.

  • Wireframes and UX flows mapped
  • UI system and interactive prototypes
  • Feedback loops with your team
04

Development

Our engineers ship in short, visible cycles — so you always know exactly where the build stands.

  • Agile sprints with weekly demos
  • Code reviews and CI/CD from day one
  • Direct collaboration with your team
05

Testing & QA

Nothing ships until it's been pushed harder than your users ever will.

  • Automated and manual test coverage
  • Performance and security checks
  • UAT sign-off before release
06

Launch & Support

Launch is the start, not the finish — we stay on to monitor, support, and iterate.

  • Staged rollout with live monitoring
  • Documentation and full handover
  • Ongoing support and iteration
