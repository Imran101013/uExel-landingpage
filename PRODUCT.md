# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing codebase: a single self-contained static HTML file (`uexel-landing.html`) with inline `<style>` and `<script>`, no framework, no build step, no backend.

## Users

Prospective B2B clients evaluating uExel as a software development partner. Healthcare IT is the primary specialty and focus; startups and enterprises across other domains (software development, AI & automation) are also served, but healthcare is the lead vertical.

## Product Purpose

uExel is a software consulting and product engineering partner. It builds web, mobile, and AI-powered products for clients, with particular depth in healthcare software (compliant infrastructure, lab information systems, EHR integrations) alongside general software development and AI/automation work for startups and enterprises.

## Positioning

Ships fast without sacrificing reliability ("ships fast without breaking things"), paired with healthcare-specific regulatory/compliance depth (HIPAA compliance, EHR integrations, lab information systems) that a generalist dev shop would not credibly claim.

## Operating Context

A marketing landing page: a prospective client arrives (search, referral, or direct), scans the hero/stats, browses "We Work With" (startup/enterprise framing), "Our Expertise" (Software Development / AI & Automation / Healthcare IT), "Featured Work," and "What Clients Say," then either books a call or submits the contact form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**This is a prototype, not a live functional website** (explicitly confirmed by the user). Concretely:
- The contact form has no real success/failure handling wired up.
- Desktop nav links and some mobile nav labels don't point to real, matching destinations.
- Testimonials are attributed to uExel's own staff, not real clients.
- Featured Work case studies use placeholder names (ABC/DEF/GHI/etc.).

Future design work on this page should default to **UI/visual enhancement** (layout, typography, color, spacing, contrast, motion, composition) rather than treating the above as bugs to fix, unless the user explicitly asks for functional/content work.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "uExel" (styled as lowercase "u" + "Exel" in accent blue).
- Type pairing: "Abel" (condensed uppercase display) for headings/numerals, "Inter" for body text.
- Palette: black/near-black (`#0a0d11`, `#0b0f14`) and navy (`#0d3a5c`, `#0a2e49`) grounding, with a blue accent (`#0b7ec9` family) for CTAs and highlights.
- Rotating hero word: "We Engineer Ideas Into IMPACT./GROWTH./VALUE./REALITY." via a canvas particle-text animation.

## Evidence on Hand

**No real proof content exists yet — all of the following are placeholder/dummy content, explicitly confirmed as not real, and must not be treated as verified facts or used as a basis for inventing similar "real-sounding" claims:**
- Hero stats ("14+ years," "120+ IT professionals," "93% recurring customers," "1100+ successful projects").
- Featured Work case studies (names "ABC," "DEF," "GHI," etc., all with identical placeholder screenshots).
- Testimonials in "What Clients Say" (currently uExel's own staff quotes, not real clients).
- Footer contact details (placeholder address text, mismatched phone number).

## Product Principles

1. Healthcare IT is the differentiator and lead vertical; startups/enterprises across other domains are secondary but real audiences — don't narrow the page to healthcare-only, but don't dilute it as the headline specialty either.
2. Speed and reliability ("ships fast without breaking things") is the core value proposition across all verticals.
3. Compliance/regulatory depth (HIPAA, EHR, lab systems) is the credibility anchor specifically for the healthcare-focused audience.
4. This is a prototype: prioritize visual/UI craft and polish over backend completeness or content authenticity unless the user asks otherwise.
5. Never fabricate replacement stats, client names, or testimonials that read as more "real" than the current explicit placeholders — flag the gap instead.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal compliance target specified. Maintain general good practice (adequate contrast, semantic markup) as already applied, without treating WCAG AA as a hard, enforced constraint.
